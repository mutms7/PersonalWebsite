EastEats | Live App: https://easteats.vercel.app/ | Source: https://github.com/mutms7/easteats
TypeScript, Next.js, PostgreSQL, Claude | Oct. 2026 - Present

- Built an East Asian restaurant finder in Next.js and TypeScript, serving listings before review analysis finishes through pgmq jobs that cover up to 120 restaurants per search.
- Kept food quality independent of service and atmosphere by using Claude to extract source-linked review evidence and deterministic TypeScript to rank it against each user's taste profile.
- Avoided new Apify and Claude calls when reopening restaurant lists by restoring account-owned search snapshots from Supabase PostgreSQL, with fresh collection triggered by new searches or explicit refresh.
